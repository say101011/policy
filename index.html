Privacy Policy for WalkQuest

Effective Date: September 15, 2026

Welcome to WalkQuest. Your privacy is critically important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data when you use the WalkQuest mobile application.

1. Information We Collect and How We Use It

Pedometer & Sensor Data: WalkQuest relies on your device's built-in step counter and accelerometer sensors to track your daily steps, active duration, and estimated calories burned. All of this data is processed locally on your device. We do not transmit your health or fitness logs to any external servers.

Location Data: WalkQuest does not track, collect, or request your GPS location.

2. Data Storage

Your walking records, personal bests, and achievements stay strictly on your device. We do not provide cloud syncing or online community leaderboards. Because data is stored locally, uninstalling the app without backing up will permanently delete your records.

3. Third-Party Services (Advertisements)

We use Google AdMob to display banner and optional rewarded video advertisements. AdMob may collect and use Google Play privacy-safe identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID) to provide personalized or non-personalized ads based on your preferences. You can manage your ad preferences or opt out of personalized ads through your Android device's system settings.

For more information on how Google uses data, please review the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Data Deletion & User Rights

You have full control over your data. You can permanently purge all of your local walking logs, step streaks, and achievements at any time by using the 'Delete Data' button within the app's Privacy Settings. This action securely wipes the data from your device and cannot be undone.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact the developer via the support email provided on the Google Play Store listing.
