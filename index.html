# WalkQuest Privacy Policy Effective September 26, 2026 WalkQuest is developed and operated by momo world. For support or privacy-related inquiries, please contact us at: say101011@gmail.com ## Walking and fitness information WalkQuest uses the device step sensor or accelerometer to count steps. After you choose Start tracking and grant applicable permissions, tracking may continue while the app is closed or the screen is off. A foreground-service notification indicates tracking. Stop tracking in Settings or disable sensor access in Privacy & Data. Step counts, history, goals, stride settings and achievements are processed and stored on the device. This app does not upload walking records to our servers or to AdMob. It does not request GPS location. ## Advertising and Google services Google AdMob provides banner advertisements. Its SDK may process IP addresses (including inferred approximate location), advertising and app identifiers, app/ad interactions and diagnostic information for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Google SDK connections use encryption in transit. This processing is separate from locally stored walking records. Consent and privacy options are presented where required through Google's User Messaging Platform. Open Privacy & Data to review available advertising choices. Non-personalized advertising can still involve device identifiers or storage. See [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [Google advertising technologies](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads) for Google's processing, retention and controls. ## Retention, deletion and export Local activity records remain until you delete them using Delete Data, clear the app's storage, or uninstall it. Delete Data clears walking records and achievements while retaining app settings. Tracking can create new records until disabled. Export creates a CSV at a location you choose; previously exported copies are not removed by in-app deletion. Local deletion does not delete information held by Google. Use Google's privacy controls for that information. There is no WalkQuest account or cloud-sync service in this version. ## Permissions and estimates Physical activity permission supports step measurement; notification permission supports visible tracking and goal alerts. Distance, calories and active time are estimates based on steps and settings. WalkQuest is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent medical conditions. ## Contact and changes Developer: momo world Support Email: say101011@gmail.com We will update this Privacy Policy when our data practices or app features change.
